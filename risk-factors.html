Dementia Risk Factors

What the research says • NSCI 140 · Group 31

Some things that raise dementia risk are out of our control, like age and genes. But the 2024 Lancet Commission found 14 risk factors that we can change, and many of them show up in midlife.

45%

of dementia cases could be prevented or delayed if all 14 risk factors were removed (Livingston et al., 2024).

Midlife (about 40 to 65)

Ten of the 14 factors belong to midlife, including the two largest: hearing loss and high LDL cholesterol. That is why this tracker is built for middle-aged adults.

  • Hearing loss
    7% of cases

    Tracker habit: Check in on your health. Hearing checks; use hearing aids if needed.

  • High LDL cholesterol New in 2024
    7% of cases

    Tracker habit: Check in on your health. Cholesterol checks from about age 40.

  • Depression
    3% of cases

    Tracker habit: Connect with someone · Take a calming break. Talk to a doctor if low mood lasts.

  • Traumatic brain injury
    3% of cases

    Wear a helmet for cycling and contact sports.

  • Physical inactivity
    2% of cases

    Tracker habit: Move for 30 minutes.

  • Diabetes
    2% of cases

    Tracker habit: Move for 30 minutes · Check in on your health.

  • Smoking
    2% of cases

    Quitting at any age helps.

  • High blood pressure
    2% of cases

    Tracker habit: Check in on your health · Move for 30 minutes.

  • Obesity
    1% of cases

    Tracker habit: Move for 30 minutes.

  • Excessive alcohol
    1% of cases

    Drink less, or add your own habit.

Later life

  • Social isolation
    5% of cases

    Tracker habit: Connect with someone.

  • Air pollution
    3% of cases

    Mostly a policy issue; limit exposure where you can.

  • Untreated vision loss New in 2024
    2% of cases

    Tracker habit: Check in on your health. Regular eye exams and glasses.

What Helps Most

The Commission’s recommendations include:

  • Use hearing aids if you have hearing loss.
  • Check and treat high LDL cholesterol from about age 40.
  • Get your eyes checked and treat vision loss.
  • Keep blood pressure in a healthy range from about age 40.
  • Stay physically, mentally, and socially active through midlife and beyond.

How to Read These Numbers

Each percentage is a population attributable fraction: the share of dementia cases worldwide that might not happen if that one factor were removed. The 14 add up to about 45%.

These are estimates for whole populations, not your personal risk. They assume each factor truly causes dementia and could be fully removed. Having a risk factor does not mean you will get dementia, and avoiding all of them does not guarantee you won’t.

References
